Privacy Policy — Ochre

Last updated 4 August 2026 · Tiếng Việt

Ochre does not collect any data about you. Not your prompts, not your photos, not your generated images, not usage statistics, not an identifier of any kind. This is not a promise about how we handle your data — the app has no way to send it anywhere.

How the app works

Ochre runs its image model entirely on your iPhone. The neural network is included in the app download, which is why the app is large. Generating and editing images requires no server and no internet connection — the app works identically in airplane mode.

The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software-development kits that transmit data.

What stays on your device

  • Your conversations and generated images, saved in the app's private storage area on the device.
  • Photos you attach for editing. They are read, processed on the device, and never transmitted.
  • Your settings, such as output size, language, and how many images you have created.

None of this is readable by us or by anyone else. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Photo library access

If you choose to save an image, Ochre asks for permission to add photos to your library. This permission is add-only: the app can save the image you asked it to save, and cannot read, browse, or delete your existing photos. Photos you attach for editing are provided by the system picker, which hands the app only the specific image you selected.

Purchases

Ochre offers a single one-time purchase that removes the trial limit. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists; we never see your name, email, payment details, or Apple Account. Apple's own handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Ochre collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no accounts, no messaging, no user-generated content shared with others, and no advertising.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. Your data is on your device and under your control: delete individual images in the app, or delete the app to remove everything at once.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, that will be stated here plainly and before the feature ships.
