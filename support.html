Common questions

Why is the app so large?
The image model itself is inside the app — about 3.6 GB of it. Nothing is downloaded afterwards, which is what lets Ochre work with no internet connection at all.
Which iPhones can run it?
iOS 18 or later, and roughly 4 GB of free memory. iPhone 15 Pro or newer is recommended. On a device with less memory, choose a smaller output size in Settings.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. Turn on airplane mode and everything behaves identically. The app never makes a network request.
Why is generation slow?
A full image model is running on your phone. The first image after launching takes longest because the model has to load. Smaller sizes and the "Fast" quality setting both speed things up considerably.
My prompt was refused. Why?
Ochre screens prompts on the device and screens each finished image again before showing it. Refusals cover explicit content, graphic violence, self-harm, hate symbols, firearms, drug use, drinking, smoking, and images of real public figures. Refusals happen entirely on your device — nothing about the request is sent anywhere. If an ordinary prompt is refused, please email the exact wording; that is the most useful bug report we can get.
Is anything limited in the free version?
There is no cap on how many images you can create, and no account is required. Two things are reserved for the one optional purchase: free images carry a small "Ochre AI" mark, and free output goes up to 512×512. The purchase removes the mark and unlocks the Large and Max sizes, up to 1024×1024.
I paid but my images still have the watermark.
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases, then check the watermark switch in the same screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry across your devices and survive reinstalling.
Can I turn off the watermark?
Yes, after the one-time purchase — the toggle is in Settings. Invisible provenance metadata marking the image as AI-generated is always written, whether the visible watermark is on or off.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual images in the library, or delete the app. Everything is stored only on your device, so removing the app removes all of it.
